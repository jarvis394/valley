# Form 2: Two galleries

Study 2 (photo islands vs grid). [Evaluation plan](../evaluation-plan.md#study-2-gallery-value-photo-islands-vs-grid). 20 or more participants, about 10 minutes. Nobody needs to use the editor, so anyone can take part, including Form 1 participants who are short on time.

Microsoft Forms settings: "Anyone can respond", don't record names. Every question is required unless marked optional.

Munich has islands, Stockholm is a grid. Both links currently return 404; publish both before the pilot.

## Start

**Title:** Valley: two photo galleries

**Description:** the [short version of the privacy notice](../privacy-notice.md#short-version-for-the-start-of-each-form), with "It takes about 10 minutes: a few questions about you, a look at two photo galleries, and questions about them." as the second sentence. Replace "A random code links your answers to what you do in Valley" with "We don't link your answers to anything else", unless the viewer log is turned on.

## C. Comparison

**Section description:** "Now you can open the galleries again. For each question, choose the middle if they are on the same level."

All are **Rating, 5 levels, number**, with 1 = Munich (first) and 5 = Stockholm (second).

| ID  | Question                                                                          |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| C1  | Which photographer is more professional?                                          |
| C2  | Which photographer presented their work the best?                                 |

| C4  | I wanted to keep looking at the photos in gallery...                              |
