## 4. Purpose

The data is used only to answer the research questions of the thesis: whether people can make a gallery they are proud of with Valley, whether grouping photos into moments helps viewers, and whether photographers can host Valley themselves.

## 5. Legal basis

Scientific research in the public interest: GDPR article 6(1)(e) and section 4 of the Finnish Data Protection Act (1050/2018). Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason.
